## App Name
**Fazoo** — Field Operations Platform

## Short Description (30 chars)
Field-force management

## Full Description

Fazoo is a field-force management platform for Brand Ambassadors and administrators.

**For Brand Ambassadors:**
- Check in at assigned stores with GPS verification
- Photograph stock on shelf and uniform selfie
- Record SKU sales in real-time
- Work offline with automatic sync when connected
- View daily history and sales summary

**For Administrators:**
- Monitor attendance and sales across all territories
- Manage brand ambassadors, stores, and campaigns
- Generate filtered reports with CSV export
- Review audit logs for compliance
- Approve or reject new registrations

**Security:**
- GPS geofence verification for check-in/checkout
- Role-based access control
- Private photo storage with signed URLs
- Full audit trail of all mutations
- Organization-scoped data isolation

## App Store Review Notes

This app is used by authorized field-force representatives (Brand Ambassadors) of registered organizations. All users require administrator approval before accessing the app. GPS location is used solely for verifying store visit compliance. Photos are taken for stock verification and uniform compliance.

The app requires:
- Camera (for stock photos and uniform selfie)
- Location (for GPS geofence verification at stores)
- Internet connection (for syncing data to the server)
